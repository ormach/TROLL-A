How it works


Damage from dice

Whenever a die is rolled in this game if the roll is [1] or [6], you take 1 damage. When your life reaches 0 you die.

How to win

Get to the last floor or the TROLL-A oil platform. Each time you resolve an event card you move to the higher floor.

Event cards

At the start of each turn you pick one of two event card. Then all your dice is rerolled and you reveal the event actions.
